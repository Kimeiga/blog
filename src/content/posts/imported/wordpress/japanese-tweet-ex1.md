<p class="wp-block-paragraph">R18 = Rated 18 or older</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">いらすと = illustration</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">垢 = アカウント but Japanese twitter people shorten it to 垢 because its あか and it saves a character.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">とか = thinks like this</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">鍵掛けてて = when your key is locked</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">「申請してください」= please apply</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">みたいなのあるじゃないですか。 = it&#8217;s like that right?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">アレって = that (is)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">中々難しいですよね = it&#8217;s pretty difficult.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">普通の垢なら = if it&#8217;s a normal account.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">「興味があって～」 = &#8220;I have a bit of interest&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">ぐらいの感覚 = about the feeling (of)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">ですけど = but</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">R18表示されてる = is labeled R18</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">以上 = since (it is)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">「たのもう！ = old way to say hello</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">我こそ = old official way to say I</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">貴方 = anata = old way to write you</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">ド = intensifier (from Dreadnought ship because Japanese measured their ships as &#8230;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">スケベ = pervert</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">アート = art</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">興味 = interest</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">津々 = overflowing (very)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">丸 = maru, a common character at the end of people&#8217;s names in olden times</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">申す = my name is (old)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">って = quotation marks</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">道場破り = to break into a dojo (and beat everyone up and best them and declare that dojo defeated by yours)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">感 = (that whole thing before)-feeling</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">満載 = being packed with</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">じゃん = じゃない shorthand</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">申請しますけど = I&#8217;m just gonna apply anyways </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">note: there&#8217;s a japanese twitter thing where to be funny you don&#8217;t put the pronunciation and just make a larger runon sentence (aka why 申請しますけど doesn&#8217;t have a period before it.</p>
