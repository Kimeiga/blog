<p><b><u><br /></u></b><b><u><br /></u></b><b><u>First why blog at all?</u></b></div>
<div><b><u><br /></u></b></div>
<p>You should make a blog if you already enjoy writing because you have a lot of thoughts and you would like to share then with the world.</p>
<div></div>
<div>I knew I would want one long ago, so I made <a href="https://www.blogger.com/blogger.g?blogID=7280731937443870496" target="_blank">kimeiga.github.io/blog</a>, a blog written with the Jekyll framework (a static site generator in Ruby) that took a long time to customize and set up but had been a long of fun to make and write on. </div>
<div></div>
<div><b>Problem with creating your own blog site from scratch:</b></div>
<div></div>
<div>I like that blog because I can customize everything about it. But I often get inspiration on the go, and I sometimes run into bugs with Jekyll as it is a complicated framework. I also have to find an image with every post for the theme I created on that site. </div>
<div></div>
<div>Sometimes I just wanna write. </div>
<div></div>
<div>So I thought, since I often get inspiration on the go, I&#8217;ll just find a free blogging service that had a nice app that I can use. </div>
<div></div>
<div><b><u>WordPress?</u></b></div>
<div></div>
<div>I tried WordPress but I didn&#8217;t like how there are ads on your site and you can&#8217;t get rid of them for free. Nothing against WordPress trying to keep itself free, but I like to avoid ads on anything I make if I can help it. </div>
<div></div>
<div><b><u>Ghost?</u></b></div>
<div><b><u><br /></u></b></div>
<div>Couldn&#8217;t find a good free plan :&#8217;c&#8230;</div>
<div></div>
<div><b><u>Write.as?</u></b></div>
<div><b><u><br /></u></b></div>
<div>Really neat idea to allow people to blog without an account and completely anonymously. But the app is not very feature rich, and the blogging platform itself doesn&#8217;t have much depth. </div>
<div></div>
<div><b><u>Blogger?</u></b></div>
<div></div>
<div>Then I tried Blogger. Apparently I&#8217;ve been a member since 2011! Back when I was &#8220;Tarin Miller&#8221; online.</div>
<div></div>
<div>I downloaded the app and it worked flawlessly although a little feature sparce. </div>
<div></div>
<div>I then downloaded the app Blogger Pro and it&#8217;s actually <i>really good. </i></div>
<div></div>
<div>I like how in Blogger they&#8217;ve added a lot of new themes that actually look nice. I have a soft spot in my heart for the old Blogger themes as they remind me of web design back when it was easy because CSS was pretty limited and people didn&#8217;t expect much. </div>
<div></div>
<div>I want to use a dark theme for the sake of everyone&#8217;s eyes but the Soho pink theme caught my eye so I might try that one out again sometime haha. </div>
<div></div>
<div>It&#8217;s a pretty good experience so far. </div>
<div></div>
<div><b><u>Closing</u></b></div>
<div></div>
<div>Why should you make a blog with Blogger?</div>
<div>
<ol>
<li>Have thought that you want to write down while somewhere with only your phone</li>
<li>Write it down in Blogger Pro (identical exclusive to writing it in a native blogging app)</li>
<li>Tap &#8220;publish&#8221; and now it&#8217;s on your website, and is shareable. </li>
</ol>
<div>Why not just use social media? Because here you own the website and it&#8217;s theme and have your own comments section powered by Google (where everyone has an account anyways). On social media people will notice you but it&#8217;s not the same experience as if they read it on your website with your theme and longform prose with images and text decoration etc.</div>
</div>
<div></div>
<div>You can always share your post to social media with a memorable snippet and a link to the rest of the article after it. It might help to include an image in the article so the link preview will have an image too. </div>
<div></div>
<div>Thanks for reading! Goodbye 💛</div>
